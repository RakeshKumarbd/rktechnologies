Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RKTechnologies ("we", "us") handles information in the apps we publish, including My Journey: Route & Chat and Nova R Play. By using our apps, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what an app needs to work:

  • Account information — such as your name and email address when you sign in.
  • Location — in My Journey, your location while you record a route, used to draw the route and show distance, time and speed.
  • Messages — in My Journey, chat messages you send to other users, used to deliver them.
  • Information you enter — for example, journey entries, notes and course progress you save in the app.
  • Device information — basic technical data such as device model and OS version, used to fix crashes and improve the app.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the app's features.
  • To fix bugs and improve performance.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app (such as hosting or crash reporting), only as needed, or when required by law.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your choices

You can delete your data by deleting it in the app or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of any data we hold, email us at the address below.

6. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
